# Project overview

**Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL)** is an independent research initiative by Md Naim Hassan Saykat that proposes how Bangladesh could build a trustworthy capability to find, analyse and cite official evidence for public policy, in Bangla and English, using AI under strong governance.

## Status in one paragraph

BAPIL is a **proposal**. No institution has been established, no host institution selected, no governance body constituted and no funding requested or approved. What exists is a set of publications, a small tested research prototype and the materials to rebuild them. The Technical Blueprint and its companions are prepared for repository release v1.1.0, which has **not** been created; the Blueprint has no DOI yet.

## What the Blueprint proposes

A university-anchored lab under an MoU with the ICT Division, with a Steering Board, an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee and a Technical Architecture Board; a bilingual, citation-grounded policy search and answer service built on documents the state has already published; six pilots under risk tiers and lifecycle gates; a 36-month plan with an indicative budget of about Tk 20.4 crore (about Tk 22.2 crore with known omissions and a 10% contingency); a strategic roadmap to 2041; and six decisions requested from the authorities. See [`governance-framework.md`](governance-framework.md), [`technical-architecture.md`](technical-architecture.md), [`evaluation-methodology.md`](evaluation-methodology.md) and [`financial-model.md`](financial-model.md).

## Intended audience

Officials in the ICT Division, the Planning Commission, the Finance Division and data-custodian agencies; universities that might host the lab; development partners; researchers in Bangla language technology and public-sector AI governance; and civil society.
