# Draft letter of intent: ICT Division (with a2i where relevant)

> **Status: draft template for discussion.** It has not been sent, signed or agreed by the ICT Division, a2i or any other government body. It shows, in the form of a letter the Division could issue, what decisions D1 to D6 of the Blueprint (Table 57) would mean in practice. Officials can adapt it, shorten it or replace it with their own wording. The text in «angle quotes» is for the Division to complete. The letter is **non-binding** and does not commit public funds. Any commitment would follow the Government's own approval procedures.

---

**Subject: Letter of intent on the proposed Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL) pilot**

1. The ICT Division has received the BAPIL Technical Blueprint (October 2026), which proposes a university-hosted lab for AI-assisted policy research, responsible AI governance and Bangla-language technology.

2. **Technical input (Decision D1).** The Division will consider the Blueprint as one technical input to the drafting of the national AI policy. This is not an endorsement of its contents.

3. **Focal point (part of D5).** The Division nominates «name, designation» as its focal point for discussions on the proposed pilot. «a2i: name, designation, if a2i takes part.»

4. **Phase 0, MoU and host (D5, D4, D2).** Subject to its own approval procedures, the Division intends to:
   - consider funding, or seeking a development partner to fund, the preparatory Phase 0 proposed in the Blueprint (about Tk 0.44 crore over about six months), which covers the open host call, an independent legal review, an independent feasibility and costing study and the confirmation of data access (Section 15.1);
   - take part in the selection of a host institution through that open expression-of-interest process (Blueprint Section 5.2);
   - after Phase 0, discuss a Memorandum of Understanding with the selected host (D2);
   - consider nominating «one or two» members of the Steering Board, including the co-chair proposed in the Blueprint (Annex G);
   - support an open call for members of the Ethics and Risk Review Committee, run by an independent nominations panel.

5. **Access to public documents (D3).** The Division will «write to / convene» the custodians of already-published laws, gazettes, budget documents and plans. These include the Legislative and Parliamentary Affairs Division, the Finance Division, the Planning Commission and the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics. The aim is to ask them to confirm the reuse terms for structured access to those collections and to nominate data custodians. No access to non-public data is implied.

6. **Funding (D5, D2).** The Division notes the indicative Phase 0 cost of about Tk 0.44 crore, the Year 1 budget of about Tk 4.7 crore and the 36-month estimate of about Tk 20.4 crore in the Blueprint. It will «examine which funding route, if any, is appropriate: a government allocation under the applicable approval procedures, a development-partner agreement, or both». A decision on Year 1 funding would follow Phase 0. This letter does not commit or allocate funds.

7. **Safeguards (D6).** The Division notes the following guardrails proposed in the Blueprint:
   - BAPIL advises and does not decide;
   - no system moves beyond risk class A without a G4 decision;
   - the prohibited uses apply, including the surveillance of individuals;
   - BAPIL does not operate production government systems.

   The Division expects these guardrails to be adopted in the Charter.

8. **Status.** This letter expresses intent only. It does not create a statutory body, give BAPIL legal powers, establish government posts or create any financial obligation for the Government.
