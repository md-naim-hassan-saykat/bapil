# Draft letter of commitment and expression of interest: candidate host institution

> **Status: draft template for discussion.** It has not been sent, signed or agreed by any institution. The author wrote it so that a university considering hosting BAPIL can adapt it. The text in «angle quotes» is for the institution to complete. The letter is **non-binding**. Under Section 5.2 of the Blueprint, the host is chosen through an open expression-of-interest process scored by an independent panel. This letter is the "signed letter of commitment" that Table 13 asks for as evidence. It does not select a host.

**Subject: Expression of interest in hosting the Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL) pilot, and letter of commitment to the BAPIL Charter**

Dear «Sir or Madam»,

1. «Name of institution» expresses its interest in hosting the proposed Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL) for its 12-month Phase 1 pilot. BAPIL is described in the BAPIL Technical Blueprint (October 2026). We have read the Blueprint, the draft Charter (Annex E) and the terms of reference of the governance bodies (Annexes F and G).

2. **Commitment to the Charter.** If selected, we would adopt the BAPIL Charter through «the competent body, for example the Syndicate or Board of Trustees». This includes its commitments on open publication, open-source software, the public-data-first policy, the risk tiers and gates, and the prohibited uses. We confirm that the independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee can exercise its veto on BAPIL projects within our statutes.

3. **What we would provide.** Subject to an agreed host agreement, we would:
   - (a) host BAPIL as a «centre / unit» with a separate cost centre and its own accounts (Blueprint Section 17.8);
   - (b) employ BAPIL staff under our own rules, on fixed-term contracts linked to the funded period. These would not be government posts, and the budget would carry the full cost of employment (Section 6.3);
   - (c) provide office space for «number» staff, and «server room / data-centre space» or a route to the National Data Center, so that non-public data is hosted in Bangladesh;
   - (d) provide finance, procurement, audit, human resources and research-ethics services, at an overhead rate of «rate» % of direct costs. The Blueprint assumes 10%;
   - (e) give access to «departments, for example computer science, linguistics, law, economics, public policy» and to graduate students for the fellowship programme.

4. **Evidence against the selection criteria** (Blueprint Table 13). Attached:
   - «publications, datasets and projects in AI, NLP and Bangla language technology»;
   - «research ethics committee and data protection arrangements»;
   - «audited accounts for the last «n» years and experience with public or development-partner grants»;
   - «infrastructure and security»;
   - «inclusion and outreach outside Dhaka»;
   - «convening facilities».

5. **Conditions.** This letter does not commit funds or staff and does not create a legal obligation. A commitment would follow only after:
   - (i) selection through the open process in Phase 0;
   - (ii) the Phase 0 legal review, including whether our statutes allow the ERRC veto;
   - (iii) an MoU between the host and the ICT Division;
   - (iv) a host agreement approved by our «competent body»;
   - (v) confirmed Year 1 funding (about Tk 4.7 crore in the Blueprint's indicative budget).

   We note that BAPIL is a proposal, and that no funding has been requested or approved.

6. **Contact.** Our focal point for this expression of interest is «name, designation, email, telephone».
