# Checklist for Bangladesh legal counsel

> **Status: draft scope of work.** It has not been sent and no counsel has been engaged. It turns Blueprint Section 14.3 and the legal items in Annex V (U1 to U3, U15) into questions that counsel can answer from the gazetted texts. Under the revised Blueprint, this review is commissioned in Phase 0 (Decision D5), before the host agreement is signed, and is published.

## 1. What has already been established (22 October 2026)

Counsel should confirm or correct each point.

| Point | Source in the Blueprint |
|---|---|
| The Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 is Act No. 63 of 2026. The National Data Management Act, 2026 is Act No. 80 and the Cyber Security Act, 2026 is Act No. 81. All three were gazetted on 10 April 2026 | Legislative Division list of 2026 Acts; Gazette copies published by the ICT Division |

The provisions were read through database texts, because the Gazette copies use a legacy Bangla font encoding. Counsel should check them against the Gazette.

## 2. Questions for counsel

**A. Status of the law** (U1, U3)

1. Confirm the numbers, Gazette dates and commencement provisions above.
2. Have the deferred sections of the Personal Data Protection Act (s. 23, ss. 31 to 35) been notified? Have any rules or regulations been issued under the three Acts?
3. Does the Personal Data Protection Act carry over the data-residency changes made by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Ordinance, 2026 (Ordinance No. 23 of 2026)? If so, which data must be kept in Bangladesh, and in what form?
4. What is the status and content of the further amendment to the Cyber Security Act? Would its content-removal or blocking powers affect BAPIL's information-integrity research (pilot P6)?

**B. BAPIL's position under the Acts**

5. For each pilot, is BAPIL, the host institution or the data-providing agency the data fiduciary (controller) or processor? The pilots are listed in Blueprint Table 38: P1 policy search, P2 budget data, P3 consultation feedback, P4 benchmark and model, P5 readiness observatory and P6 information-integrity research.
6. Which exemptions apply to research and statistics, and under what conditions? Does processing of already-published official documents (P1, P2) raise any duty under the Personal Data Protection Act?
7. Must BAPIL or its host appoint a chief data officer under the National Data Management Act, or register with the National Data Management Authority?
8. Is NRDEX operating? What agreements does it require for access to non-public data (Annex I)?
9. Does publishing open datasets and benchmarks under CC BY 4.0 raise any issue? This includes text from government documents and the Right to Information Act, 2009.

**C. Institution and employment** (U15)

10. Can the governing body of the selected host lawfully accept a binding veto by an external Ethics and Risk Review Committee (Annex F)? If not, what form of words in the host agreement would come closest?
11. Is the employment model in Section 6.3 consistent with the host's service rules and with national law? The model uses fixed-term contracts linked to funding, the host's provident fund, and the Universal Pension System.
12. Do any approvals beyond the MoU, the host agreement and a funding agreement apply? Examples are the ICT Division's own procedures, Economic Relations Division clearance for foreign grants, and procurement rules (Annex T, Table 65).

**D. Documents to review against the findings**

13. Draft Charter (Annex E), terms of reference (Annexes F and G), data classification (Chapter 8), data-sharing agreement outline (Annex I), DPIA template (Annex H), compliance checklist (Table 43).
