# BAPIL: one-page briefing note

**Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL): a proposal for a 12-month pilot.** Md Naim Hassan Saykat, Independent Researcher, Bangladesh. October 2026. Draft for discussion.

**The proposal.** BAPIL is a proposed lab, hosted by a university and linked to government through an MoU. Its job is to help Bangladesh use artificial intelligence for evidence-based policy, responsible AI governance and Bangla-language technology. It does not make policy, regulate or run production government systems. No institution has been created, and no funding has been requested or approved.

**Why now.** A draft National AI Policy 2026-2030 went to consultation in January 2026, with a revised draft in February 2026. As reported, national AI policy drafting is to begin in Q4 2026. The 2026 data protection and data management laws create new duties for anyone who handles public data, although which provisions are in force is still to be confirmed. An open, tested blueprint can serve as a technical input.

**First-year deliverables (Phase 1 pilot).**

- **P1, Policy Search.** Bilingual search over laws, gazettes, plans and budget documents. Every answer sentence cites an official source, and the system declines to answer when the evidence is weak. Targets:
  - Recall@10 of at least 0.85;
  - citation precision of at least 0.95;
  - usefulness of at least 4.0 out of 5 from 30 or more users.
- **P2, Budget Intelligence.** Five fiscal years of ministry-level allocations, published as open data, with at least 99% accuracy on a checked sample.
- **BanglaPolicyQA version 0.** An open benchmark of 500 questions.
- **Training.** 100 officials in Year 1.
- **Governance.** A Steering Board, an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee (ERRC) and a public transparency register.

**Cost and jobs.**

| Item | Year 1 | 36 months |
|----------|----------|------------------------------------------|
| Indicative budget | About Tk 4.7 crore | About Tk 20.4 crore. The range is Tk 17.9 to 25.3 crore. Known omissions and a 10% contingency would bring it to about Tk 22.2 crore |
| Posts | 9 | 19 by Year 3, plus 28 graduate fellowships |

Funds are released in three annual tranches, each after a review, so only about 23% is committed by the end of Year 1. A conditional national scale-up in Years 4 to 8 is costed at about Tk 218.9 crore, reaching about 118 posts. It is not requested now, and it would follow only if the pilots pass their gates.
