# BAPIL: one-page briefing note

**Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL): a proposal for a preparatory Phase 0 and a 12-month pilot.** Md Naim Hassan Saykat, Independent Researcher, Bangladesh. October 2026. Draft for discussion.

**The proposal.** BAPIL is a proposed lab, hosted by a university and linked to government through an MoU. Its job is to help Bangladesh use artificial intelligence for evidence-based policy, responsible AI governance and Bangla-language technology. It does not make policy, regulate or run production government systems. No institution has been created, and no funding has been requested or approved.

**Why now.** A draft National AI Policy 2026-2030 went to consultation in January 2026 (revised in February), and national AI policy drafting is reported to begin in Q4 2026. The Personal Data Protection Act and the National Data Management Act, gazetted on 10 April 2026 (some provisions await notification), create new duties for anyone who handles public data.

**Phase 0 (about six months, about Tk 0.44 crore).** An open call to select a host university, an independent legal review, an independent feasibility and costing study, and confirmation of data access. No pilot money is committed until Phase 0 is complete.

**First-year deliverables (Phase 1 pilot).**

- **P1, Policy Search.** Bilingual search over laws, gazettes, plans and budget documents. Every answer sentence cites an official source, and the system declines to answer when the evidence is weak. Targets:
  - Recall@10 of at least 0.85;
  - citation precision of at least 0.95;
  - usefulness of at least 4.0 out of 5 from 30 or more users.
- **P2, Budget Intelligence.** Five fiscal years of ministry-level allocations, published as open data, with at least 99% accuracy on a checked sample.
- **BanglaPolicyQA version 0.** An open benchmark of 500 questions.
- **Training and governance.** 100 officials trained in Year 1; a Steering Board, an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee (ERRC) and a public transparency register.

**Cost and jobs.**

| Item | Year 1 | 36 months |
|----------|----------|------------------------------------------|
| Indicative budget | About Tk 4.7 crore | About Tk 20.4 crore (range Tk 17.9 to 25.3 crore; about Tk 22.2 crore with known omissions and a 10% contingency) |
| Posts | 9 | 19 by Year 3, plus 28 graduate fellowships |

Funds are released in three annual tranches, each after a review, so only about 23% is committed by the end of Year 1. Year 1 relies on the government budget (about 70%) and one development partner. A conditional national scale-up in Years 4 to 8 (about Tk 218.9 crore, about 118 posts) is not requested now and would follow only if the pilots pass their gates.
