# Stakeholder consultation plan: Q4 2026 to Q2 2027

This is a working plan prepared by the author. It turns Section 21.3 of the Blueprint ("Immediate next steps") and decisions D1 to D6 (Table 57) into a sequence of meetings, documents and records. Nothing in it has taken place yet. Dates are targets, and none of them depends on public funds.

## 1. Objectives

1. Present the Blueprint as a technical input to national AI policy drafting (D1).
2. Find at least two candidate host institutions willing to answer an open call (Section 5.2, Table 13).
3. Secure funding for Phase 0 and a government focal point (D5), and agree on the route to an MoU after Phase 0 (D2).
4. Confirm reuse terms for already-public document collections with their custodians (D3).
5. Test the main assumptions with experts and the public, and publish what changed (Table 8; Blueprint 1.1). The assumptions to test are:
   - the budget;
   - the staffing;
   - the risk tiers;
   - the evaluation targets.
6. Collect the evidence that the open items in Annex V need. These items cover:
   - the laws in force (U1 to U3);
   - the budget inputs from the host (U6);
   - the host, governance bodies and focal point (U9);
   - an independent Bangla language review (U14);
   - the veto within host statutes (U15);
   - the omitted costs (U16).

## 4. Principles

- **Accurate status.** Every meeting and document makes clear that BAPIL is a proposal and that no funding has been requested or approved. Nothing is described as agreed until it is in writing.
- **Records.** Each meeting has a short note of who attended, what was asked and what was agreed. It is kept by the author and shared with participants. Personal contact details are not published.
- **Bilingual.** Material for Bangladeshi bodies is offered in Bangla and English.
- **Inclusion.** At least one roundtable and part of the outreach take place outside Dhaka or online, and women experts are actively invited. These follow the inclusion criteria in Table 13 and the training target in Table 66.
- **Conflicts of interest.** Experts and candidate hosts declare any interest. A candidate host does not review the host criteria.
- **Transparency of changes.** Every change that comes from consultation is listed in the comment log and in the Blueprint's change record.
