## 2. Overall questions

Short answers are enough.

1. Is the problem BAPIL addresses real and important for Bangladesh? What evidence would you add?
2. Is the institutional model right: a lab hosted by a university, with an MoU with the ICT Division? Would another model work better in practice?
3. Are the budget (about Tk 20.4 crore over 36 months) and the staffing plan realistic? Which assumption worries you most?
4. Are the safeguards sufficient? Consider the risk tiers, the gates, the ethics committee's veto and the prohibited uses.
5. Are the first-year targets achievable? These are Recall@10 of at least 0.85, citation precision of at least 0.95, and 30 active users.
6. What is missing that a decision-maker would ask about?
7. Should the authorities take decisions D1 to D6 (Table 57)? If not, what would you change?
