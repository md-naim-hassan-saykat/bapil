## Overview

**Status:** BAPIL is a proposal by an independent researcher. No institution has been established, no host institution selected, no governance body constituted and no funding requested or approved. The repository contains publications, a small research prototype and the materials to rebuild them.

The Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL) is a research-driven initiative that explores how Artificial Intelligence (AI), data analytics, and digital technologies can support evidence-based policymaking, public sector innovation, and responsible AI governance in Bangladesh.

BAPIL aims to bridge the gap between AI research, public policy, governance, and innovation by developing frameworks, methodologies, and prototype systems that can assist decision-makers, researchers, and stakeholders in addressing national challenges through data-informed approaches.

## Inspiration & Related Initiatives

BAPIL draws inspiration from international efforts in AI governance, policy intelligence, digital government, public-sector innovation, and responsible AI adoption. The framework incorporates lessons from a diverse set of countries and institutions that have demonstrated leadership in digital transformation, AI strategy, governance, and innovation.

These include:

* Singapore Smart Nation Initiative and GovTech Singapore
* South Korea National AI Strategy and AI Hub initiatives
* Estonia’s digital government and digital identity ecosystem
* United Kingdom AI governance programs and Government Digital Service (GDS)
* Finland’s AI literacy initiatives, including the Elements of AI program
* United Arab Emirates (UAE) National AI Strategy and digital government initiatives
* Canada’s responsible AI governance frameworks and Directive on Automated Decision-Making
* OECD AI Policy Observatory (OECD.AI)
* UNESCO AI Ethics and Responsible AI initiatives
* NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)
* World Bank GovTech and Digital Development programs
* UNDP Digital Governance and Public Sector Innovation initiatives

BAPIL does not seek to replicate any existing model. Instead, it aims to synthesize international best practices and adapt relevant lessons to the Bangladeshi context, with a focus on AI governance, policy intelligence, digital transformation, public-sector innovation, Bangla-language AI, and evidence-based decision support.

The initiative is envisioned as a complementary research and policy intelligence framework that supports Bangladesh’s broader digital transformation agenda, including Smart Bangladesh Vision 2041 and emerging national AI initiatives.

## Vision

To contribute to a future where public policy, governance, and innovation in Bangladesh are supported by trustworthy, transparent, and evidence-based AI systems.

---

## Mission

- Promote AI-assisted policy intelligence and research.
- Support data-driven decision-making frameworks.
- Advance responsible AI governance and ethics.
- Encourage interdisciplinary collaboration between technology, government, academia, and industry.
- Foster innovation, talent development, and public sector transformation.

---

## Objectives

- Study international best practices in AI governance and policy intelligence.
- Develop frameworks for policy analysis and decision support.
- Explore AI applications in public administration and governance.
- Investigate methods for misinformation detection and digital resilience.
- Support AI education, research, and innovation ecosystems.
- Encourage responsible and ethical deployment of AI technologies.

---

## Scope

BAPIL focuses on research, policy intelligence, AI governance, public sector innovation, and capacity building. The initiative is intended to support discussion, analysis, and evidence-based decision-making and does not perform policymaking functions.

---

## Long-Term Vision (2026 to 2041)

BAPIL envisions a future in which Artificial Intelligence, data analytics, and evidence-based policy intelligence contribute to Bangladesh’s long-term development objectives and digital transformation goals.

By 2041, the initiative aims to support research, collaboration, and capacity-building efforts that may contribute to:

* The adoption of evidence-based and data-informed policymaking practices.
* The development of responsible AI governance and regulatory frameworks.
* AI-assisted policy intelligence and decision-support capabilities.
* Bangla-language AI research and public-interest AI applications.
* Digital resilience, cybersecurity awareness, and misinformation mitigation.
* AI talent development and workforce readiness.
* Stronger collaboration among academia, government, industry, and startups.
* International partnerships in AI governance, digital government, and emerging technologies.

BAPIL's long-term aspiration is to serve as a research and innovation platform that promotes trustworthy, ethical, and transparent AI while supporting Bangladesh’s vision of becoming a knowledge-based, technologically advanced, and innovation-driven nation by 2041.

## Core Components

The White Paper (Version 1.0) envisages the following units. They are concepts for a future institution; none of them exists. The Technical Blueprint refines them into the proposed institutional design described in [`docs/governance-framework.md`](docs/governance-framework.md).

### AI Policy & Research Unit

Research on AI-assisted policy analysis, governance frameworks, and public sector innovation.

### Data Analytics & Public Sentiment Unit

Exploration of data-driven approaches for understanding public concerns, social trends, and citizen feedback.

### Digital Security & Misinformation Lab

Research on misinformation detection, digital trust, cybersecurity awareness, and information integrity.

### AI Governance & Ethics Unit

Study of AI regulation, accountability, transparency, privacy, and ethical deployment practices.

### AI Education & Talent Development Unit

Development of educational resources, training initiatives, and AI literacy programs.

### Innovation & Startup Collaboration Cell

Investigation of opportunities for AI entrepreneurship, innovation ecosystems, and technology transfer.

## Technical Blueprint

**Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL): Technical Blueprint**, Version 1.0, October 2026  
**বাংলাদেশ এআই পলিসি অ্যান্ড ইনোভেশন ল্যাব (BAPIL): টেকনিক্যাল ব্লুপ্রিন্ট**, সংস্করণ ১.০, অক্টোবর ২০২৬

The Technical Blueprint turns the White Paper into a buildable plan. It is written in English (authoritative) and Bangla, with a short Blueprint Summary in both languages and a separate Decision Memo addressed to the authorities. The files below are prepared for repository release v1.1.0; the release and the Blueprint's Zenodo DOI do not exist yet (see [Versioning](#versioning-and-release-status)).

|---|---|---|
| Technical Blueprint (publication PDF) | [BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_en.pdf](releases/v1.1/BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_en.pdf) | [BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_bn.pdf](releases/v1.1/BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_bn.pdf) |
| Technical Blueprint (assembled Markdown) | [BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_en.md](blueprint/BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_en.md) | [BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_bn.md](blueprint/BAPIL_Technical_Blueprint_bn.md) |
| Editable master sources | [`blueprint/src/en/`](blueprint/src/en/) | [`blueprint/src/bn/`](blueprint/src/bn/) |
| Blueprint Summary | [PDF](releases/v1.1/BAPIL_Blueprint_Summary_en.pdf) · [Markdown](blueprint/BAPIL_Blueprint_Summary_en.md) | [PDF](releases/v1.1/BAPIL_Blueprint_Summary_bn.pdf) · [Markdown](blueprint/BAPIL_Blueprint_Summary_bn.md) |
| Decision Memo (separate document, not part of the Blueprint) | [PDF](releases/v1.1/BAPIL_Decision_Memo_en.pdf) · [Markdown](blueprint/BAPIL_Decision_Memo_en.md) | [PDF](releases/v1.1/BAPIL_Decision_Memo_bn.pdf) · [Markdown](blueprint/BAPIL_Decision_Memo_bn.md) |
| Budget model (editable workbook and CSV) | [BAPIL_Budget_Model.xlsx](blueprint/data/BAPIL_Budget_Model.xlsx) · [CSV](blueprint/data/) | |

The editable masters are Markdown chapter files in [`blueprint/src/`](blueprint/src/); tables are Python data (`tables_*.py`), figures are TikZ (`make_figures.py`), the budget is computed by `budget_model.py` (also exported as CSV to [`blueprint/data/`](blueprint/data/) and re-implemented with formulas in an editable workbook), and new references are in `refs_new.md`. One command rebuilds every table, figure and PDF: `blueprint/build_blueprint.sh` (tools, fonts and steps in [`blueprint/README.md`](blueprint/README.md)).

The Blueprint specifies:

1. 26 requirements traced to components, code and tests;
2. an institutional design (university-anchored lab, government MoU, Steering Board, independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee with a binding veto within the limits of the host's statutes, Technical Architecture Board);
3. a reference architecture of twelve open components hosted in Bangladesh;
4. data rules (four data classes, public data first) aligned with the data-protection and data-management framework of 2025 and 2026 as reported;
5. a bilingual, citation-grounded retrieval design that answers with citations or abstains, a planned open benchmark (BanglaPolicyQA, not yet built) and a staged, evidence-gated path to Bangla domain adaptation;
6. six pilots with users, data, risk tier, scope and exit criteria;
7. responsible-AI assurance: risk tiers T0 to T3, lifecycle gates G0 to G5, model cards and a public transparency register;
8. a 36-month delivery plan with an indicative budget (about Tk 20.4 crore in the base case, about Tk 22.2 crore with known omissions and a 10% contingency, low and high scenarios of about Tk 17.9 and 25.3 crore; planning estimates, not approved funding), a costed, conditional national scale-up for Years 4 to 8 if the pilots succeed (about Tk 218.9 crore and about 118 direct posts by Year 8; not requested now), staffing and training plan, financing and procurement clauses, logical framework, KPIs, risk register and research agenda;
9. a strategic roadmap for 2026 to 2041 and beyond (horizons, indicative targets, scenarios and review points);
10. six decisions for the authorities;
11. evidence and readiness annexes: the evidence status of key claims, budget reconciliation and scenarios, an evaluation protocol with sample sizes, the status and limits of each governance body, preconditions for starting, risk monitoring and a register of unresolved items.

The Blueprint has five parts, 21 chapters, 23 annexes, 72 tables and 16 figures (145 pages in English and 142 in Bangla). All figures and tables are also available as LaTeX, PDF and PNG in English and Bangla under [`blueprint/figures/`](blueprint/figures/) and [`blueprint/tables/`](blueprint/tables/).

**Known limitations.** Legal descriptions rest on press reports and secondary legal commentary, which sometimes disagree; the gazetted texts were not reviewed and need confirmation by qualified Bangladesh counsel ([`docs/legal-references.md`](docs/legal-references.md)). No pilot, benchmark or governance body exists yet, and all quality thresholds are proposed targets. The reference implementation is a research prototype tested only on Linux. The Bangla edition has had an editorial review by the author but not yet an independent professional copy-edit. The full list, with owners, is the unresolved-items register (Annex V, Table 68).

## Reference Implementation

[`implementation/`](implementation/) contains `bapil` version 0.1.0, a small dependency-free Python research prototype (not a deployed system) demonstrating the core components of the Blueprint: Bangla-aware normalisation, bilingual BM25 search, citation-grounded extractive answers with abstention, risk-tier assessment, an AI system register with transparency export, evaluation metrics and a hash-chained audit log, with a command line and a small JSON API.

```bash
cd implementation && pip install -e .
python -m bapil.cli ask "বাংলা নীতি ফাউন্ডেশন মডেল কেন প্রয়োজন?"
python -m unittest discover -s tests
```

The 49 unit tests pass when run locally (Python 3.13 on Linux, October 2026). The GitHub Actions workflows in `.github/workflows/` have not yet run on GitHub, so there is no public test record until the release branch is pushed.
