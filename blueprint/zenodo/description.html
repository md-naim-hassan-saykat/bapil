The Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL) Technical Blueprint turns the BAPIL White Paper (Version 1.0) into a buildable plan for an AI policy-intelligence capability in Bangladesh.

The Blueprint has five parts, 21 chapters, 22 annexes, 68 tables and 15 figures, in English and Bangla (বাংলাদেশ এআই পলিসি অ্যান্ড ইনোভেশন ল্যাব (BAPIL): টেকনিক্যাল ব্লুপ্রিন্ট). It is a proposal: no institution has been created and no funding has been approved. It specifies:

  • 26 requirements (functional, non-functional and governance), traced to components, code and tests;
  • an institutional design: a university-anchored lab with a government MoU, a multi-stakeholder Steering Board, an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee with a binding veto (within the limits of the host institution's statutes), and a Technical Architecture Board;
  • a reference architecture of twelve open, replaceable components, hosted in Bangladesh;
  • data rules: four data classes and a public-data-first policy designed for the data-protection and data-management framework of 2025 and 2026 as reported (legal descriptions to be confirmed by counsel);
  • a bilingual (Bangla and English), citation-grounded retrieval design that answers with citations or abstains, the open BanglaPolicyQA benchmark, and an evidence-gated path to Bangla domain adaptation (the White Paper's Bangla Policy Foundation Model);
  • six pilots with users, data, risk tier, scope and exit criteria;
  • a responsible-AI assurance system: risk tiers T0 to T3, lifecycle gates G0 to G5, model cards and a public transparency register, mapped to OECD, UNESCO, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act;
  • a small, tested, dependency-free Python reference implementation (a research prototype, not a production system), issued in GitHub release v1.1.0 with the Blueprint;
  • a 36-month delivery plan with an indicative budget (about Tk 20.4 crore in the base case and about Tk 22.2 crore with known omissions and a 10% contingency; planning estimates, not approved funding), staffing and training plan, financing and procurement clauses, logical framework, KPIs, risk register and research agenda;
  • a strategic roadmap for 2026 to 2041 and beyond, with short, medium and long-term horizons, indicative targets, scenarios and review points;
  • six decisions for the relevant authorities;
  • evidence and readiness annexes: the evidence status of key claims, budget reconciliation and scenarios, an evaluation protocol, governance status and safeguards, preconditions, risk monitoring and a register of unresolved items.

Files: Technical Blueprint and Blueprint Summary in English and Bangla (PDF and Markdown); fifteen figures and sixty-eight tables in LaTeX, PDF and PNG (English and Bangla); the editable source package (Markdown masters, table data, figure sources, budget model and CSV outputs, templates and build script); source archive of the reference implementation.

Companion publications: White Paper, Version 1.0 (10.5281/zenodo.21363967); Executive Summary, Version 1.0 (10.5281/zenodo.21441172).
