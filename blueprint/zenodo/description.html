The Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL) Technical Blueprint turns the BAPIL White Paper (Version 1.0) into a buildable plan for an AI policy-intelligence capability in Bangladesh.

The Blueprint has five parts, 21 chapters and 15 annexes. It specifies:

  • 26 requirements (functional, non-functional and governance), traced to components, code and tests;
  • an institutional design: a university-anchored lab with a government MoU, a multi-stakeholder Steering Board, an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee with a binding veto, and a Technical Architecture Board;
  • a reference architecture of twelve open, replaceable components, hosted in Bangladesh;
  • data rules: four data classes and a public-data-first policy aligned with the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 and the National Data Management Act, 2026;
  • a bilingual (Bangla and English), citation-grounded retrieval design that answers with citations or abstains, the open BanglaPolicyQA benchmark, and an evidence-gated path to a Bangla Policy Foundation Model;
  • six pilots with users, data, risk tier, scope and exit criteria;
  • a responsible-AI assurance system: risk tiers T0 to T3, lifecycle gates G0 to G5, model cards and a public transparency register, mapped to OECD, UNESCO, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act;
  • a tested, dependency-free Python reference implementation on GitHub;
  • a 36-month delivery plan with an indicative budget, staffing and training plan, financing and procurement clauses, logical framework, KPIs, risk register and research agenda;
  • a strategic roadmap for 2026 to 2041 and beyond, with short, medium and long-term horizons, indicative targets, scenarios and review points;
  • six decisions for the relevant authorities.

Files: Technical Blueprint, Blueprint Summary and one-page Decision Memo in English and Bangla (PDF and editable Markdown); fifteen figures and fifty-nine tables in LaTeX, PDF and PNG (English and Bangla); source archive of the reference implementation.
