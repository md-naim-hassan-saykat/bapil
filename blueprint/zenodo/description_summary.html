The Bangladesh AI Policy & Innovation Lab (BAPIL): Blueprint Summary is an eleven-page summary of the BAPIL Technical Blueprint, Version 1.0 (10.5281/zenodo.23587901), in English and Bangla (বাংলাদেশ এআই পলিসি অ্যান্ড ইনোভেশন ল্যাব (BAPIL): ব্লুপ্রিন্ট সারসংক্ষেপ).

It covers what BAPIL is and why it is proposed now; what the Blueprint specifies; the institution and its governance; the architecture and AI design; the six pilots; the safeguards; the preparatory Phase 0 and the 36-month delivery plan; the strategic roadmap to 2041; people, resources and financing; the reference implementation and research agenda; and the six decisions requested from the authorities.

BAPIL is a proposal: no institution has been created and no funding has been approved. Budget figures are planning estimates and all quality thresholds are proposed targets.
