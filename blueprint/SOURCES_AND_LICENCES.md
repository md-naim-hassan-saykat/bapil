## Not included

* No credentials, tokens, private keys or personal data are included. The only personal information is the author's name, ORCID and public GitHub account, which appear in the publication by the author's choice.
* The internal review records in `review/` are kept in the repository for traceability but are not part of the publication or of the Zenodo package.

## Unresolved source dependencies

* The Act numbers, Gazette dates and commencement of the 2026 Acts are confirmed from official sources (the Legislative Division list of Acts and the Gazette copies published by the ICT Division). Their provisions were read from the laws database texts, because the Gazette copies could not be read automatically, and need confirmation by counsel (Table 68, U1 to U3).
