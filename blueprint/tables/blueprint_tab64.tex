\rowcolor{white}\textbf{Body} & \textbf{Status and basis} & \textbf{Membership and appointment} & \textbf{Chair and secretariat} & \textbf{Decision rights and limits} & \textbf{Reporting and transparency} & \textbf{Independence and conflicts} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Steering Board} & Proposed; created by the MoU and the host agreement; no statutory power & 7 to 9 members nominated by their organisations; academic and civil-society members chosen by open call; 3-year terms & Co-chairs from the ICT Division and the host; secretariat in the Director's office & Approves plans, budgets, partnerships and senior appointments; joint G4 decisions with agencies; cannot override an ERRC refusal or the law & Meets quarterly; decisions and minutes published in summary; annual report & Annual declarations; recusal; the industry member does not vote on procurement in its sector \\
\textbf{Ethics and Risk Review Committee (ERRC)} & Proposed; established by the charter; binding inside BAPIL through the charter and host agreement, not by statute & 5 to 7 members, a majority external, selected through an open call by the independent nominations panel and confirmed by the Steering Board; 3-year terms, renewable once; removal only for cause by two thirds of the Board after a hearing & External chair elected by members; secretariat provided by the governance analyst & Approves, approves with conditions, refuses or suspends tier T2 and T3 work at G1, G2 and G4; refusal is binding; cannot authorise what the law or the host forbids & Decision summaries within 30 days; dissent recorded; annual report & Majority external; no member employed by a supplier under evaluation; protected budget; staff may raise concerns directly \\
\textbf{Technical Architecture Board (TAB)} & Proposed; internal technical body under the Director & Lead engineers, the security lead and two external reviewers appointed by the Director with Board approval & Chief engineer; secretariat in the platform team & Approves designs at G1, models and major dependencies; maintains ADRs & ADRs public in the repository; meets monthly & External reviewers declare commercial interests and do not review their employers' products \\
\textbf{International Advisory Panel} & Proposed; advisory only & Invited by the Steering Board; personal capacity; 2-year terms & Chair chosen by members; secretariat in the Director's office & None; gives advice on methods, benchmarks and governance & Annual note published & Declarations of interest \\
\textbf{Director} & Proposed post; employee of the host institution & Appointed by the host through open competition and confirmed by the Steering Board & Not applicable & Operational decisions within approved plans and budgets; may stop any pilot & Monthly reports to the Board & Host employment rules; declarations \\
\textbf{Host-agency product owners} & Officials designated in writing by their agencies & Designated by the agency head & Not applicable & Define needs; accept or reject pilots at G3 and G4 in their agency & Pilot reports & Act under agency rules; receive no payment from BAPIL \\
\textbf{Independent nominations panel} & Proposed; convened once for each ERRC term & Three people of standing in law, ethics and civil society, invited by the host institution's research ethics committee & Members choose a convener & Shortlists and recommends ERRC members & Publishes the call and selection criteria & Members may not serve on the ERRC they select \\
