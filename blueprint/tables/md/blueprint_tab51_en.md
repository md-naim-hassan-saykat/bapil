**Table 51.** Indicative funding mix by horizon

| Source | Year 1 (Phase 1) | H1 from Year 2 | H2 2029 to 2030 | H3 2031 to 2035 | H4 2036 to 2041 |
|-------------------------|------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| Government core contribution (MoU, development budget) | 70 to 75% | 30 to 40% | 40 to 50% | 45 to 55% | 50 to 60% |
| Competitive research grants | 0% | 20 to 30% | 15 to 25% | 10 to 20% | 10 to 15% |
| Development partners (pilot-specific) | 25 to 30% | 20 to 30% | 15 to 20% | 5 to 10% | 0 to 5% |
| Service agreements with agencies (cost recovery) | 0% | 0% | 5 to 10% | 15 to 25% | 20 to 30% |
| Industry and philanthropy (open benchmarks, fellowships) | 0% | 5 to 10% | 5 to 10% | 5 to 10% | 5 to 10% |

*Note.* Year 1 relies on sources that can be committed before Month 1: the government budget and one development partner. Research grants and industry funds follow once the lab exists. No single non-government funder should provide more than 30% of the budget in any year, and industry support never buys influence over research results or governance decisions.
