*Note.* Phase 0 runs for about six months after Decision D5. Month 1 of the plan is the first month in which the MoU, the host agreement and Year 1 funding are all in place. If D5 is taken in the fourth quarter of 2026, Phase 0 can run in the first half of 2027 and Month 1 can be July 2027, the start of FY2027-28, so that Year 1 funding can be placed in that year’s budget.
