Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 & Act No. 63 of 2026, gazetted on 10 April 2026, replacing the Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025 [7, 8]. Deemed in force from 6 November 2025, except section 23 (chief data officers) and sections 31 to 35, which come into force by Gazette notification after 18 months [42]. Rules: to be confirmed & Consent as a clear affirmative indication, with other specified grounds (s. 5); data-subject rights including withdrawal from automated decisions (s. 13); enhanced protection of sensitive and children’s data (s. 7); chief data officers (s. 23); data-residency provisions changed by the 2026 amendment ordinance [78], reportedly limited to critical sectors [79] (whether carried into the Act: to be confirmed) & DC0-first; DPIA before any DC2 work; DC3 excluded; no automated decisions about individuals; hosting in Bangladesh \\
National Data Management Act, 2026 & Act No. 80 of 2026, gazetted on 10 April 2026, succeeding the National Data Management Ordinance, 2025 (called the National Data Governance Ordinance in some English reports) [6, 7, 9]. Deemed in force from 6 November 2025 [42]. Rules: to be confirmed & National Data Management Authority (s. 8); National Responsible Data Exchange (NRDEX); a chief data officer in every institution (s. 33); national code repository for government software (s. 23) [42] & Use NRDEX agreements where available; publish code openly; coordinate with the Authority and agency data officers \\
Cyber Security Act, 2026 and amendment & Act No. 81 of 2026, gazetted on 10 April 2026, deemed in force from 21 May 2025 [7, 10, 42]. Cyber Security (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act No. 99), gazetted on 1 July 2026 with immediate effect, omits only section 20, the online gambling provision [11, 43]; it does not concern AI-generated content, contrary to an earlier report [102]. A further amendment under cabinet committee review from July 2026 was still proposed in September 2026 [44, 103] & National Cyber Security Agency (s. 5); critical information infrastructure; removal and blocking of content, subject to Tribunal approval within three days (s. 8); AI and AI-generated data named in the definitions (s. 2) & NIST CSF 2.0 controls; incident reporting; information-integrity research kept within law and rights \\
Right to Information Act, 2009 & In force [74] & Proactive disclosure of public information & Supports the DC0 corpus and the public transparency register \\
National AI policy & Draft 2026-2030 released for public consultation in late January 2026 [12, 13]; revised draft (version 2.0, 9 February 2026) posted on the official portal [14]; formulation by a joint team announced to begin in Q4 2026 [16]. Not adopted & Risk categories; algorithmic impact assessment; an independent oversight committee to be created by an Act of Parliament; Bangla LLM; national compute; AI innovation fund of Tk 200 to 250 crore to 2030 (proposed) [12] & Tiers aligned to draft categories; Blueprint offered as technical input; BPFM coordinated with national LLM plans \\
Long-term national plans & Perspective Plan 2021 to 2041 (2020) and the draft ICT Master Plan 2041 [17, 18] & Long-term digital transformation goals & Roadmap aligned to the 2041 horizon and adaptable to plan revisions \\
AI Hub and AI skills project (KOICA, ICT Division, Bangladesh Hi-Tech Park Authority) & Technical assistance project proposal of Tk 192.66 crore awaiting government approval in June 2026 [56]; USD 13 million grant agreement (Record of Discussion) reported signed in August 2026 for 2026 to 2029 [58]. Not reported as operational & Skills, startups, AI adoption & Complementary division of labour; shared benchmarks and open data \\
