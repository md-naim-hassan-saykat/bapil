\textbf{Steering Board} & 7 to 9 members: ICT Division (co-chair), host institution (co-chair), Planning Commission, a data custodian agency, academia, civil society, industry; BAPIL Director as secretary & Strategy, annual plan and budget; partnership approvals; joint G4 adoption decisions with host agencies & Approves plans and budgets \\
\textbf{Ethics and Risk Review Committee (ERRC)} & 5 to 7 members, majority external: law and human rights, data protection, ethics, domain experts, civil society, technical reviewer & Reviews tier T2 and T3 systems at G1, G2 and G4; reviews incidents; publishes decision summaries & Binding veto on pilots and deployment \\
\textbf{Technical Architecture Board (TAB)} & Lead engineers, security lead, two external technical reviewers & Architecture and security standards; design reviews; model and dependency approvals & Approves designs at G1 \\
\textbf{International Advisory Panel} & Invited experts serving in a personal capacity & Peer review of methods, benchmarks and governance; international linkages & Advisory only \\
\textbf{Director and units} & Director, six thematic units from the White Paper, and a cross-cutting platform engineering team & Delivery of research, pilots, training and publications & Operational decisions within approved plans \\
\textbf{Host-agency product owners} & Named officials in each agency piloting a tool & Define needs, recruit pilot users, accept or reject at G3 and G4 & Acceptance of pilots in their agency \\
