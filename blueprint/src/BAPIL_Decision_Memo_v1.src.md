# DECISION MEMO: A Low-Risk, 12-Month Pilot of an AI Policy-Intelligence Capability for Bangladesh

**To:** Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Information Technology (ICT Division), and relevant authorities: Planning Commission, Finance Division, Legislative and Parliamentary Affairs Division, Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, a2i, National Data Management Authority  
**From:** Md Naim Hassan Saykat, Independent Researcher (BAPIL) · **Date:** {{DATE}} · **Basis:** BAPIL Technical Blueprint, Version 1.0 ({{DOI_SHORT}})

**Purpose.** To request six decisions that would allow a small, safe and open pilot of an AI capability that helps officials find and cite official evidence in Bangla and English.

**Why now.** (1) The government has announced that drafting of a national AI policy begins in Q4 2026 [91]; a tested, openly licensed blueprint can inform it. (2) The Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 and the National Data Management Act, 2026, as reported, now set the rules for public-sector data [85, 86]; commencement of individual provisions is to be confirmed. (3) Laws, gazettes, budgets and plans are scattered across sites and formats, often as scanned Bangla PDFs, and there is no open Bangla benchmark for policy search.

**Proposal.** A university-anchored BAPIL lab under an MoU with the ICT Division, a multi-stakeholder Steering Board and an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee with a binding veto. Year 1 delivers: **P1** a bilingual policy search tool whose answers cite official sources (or abstain), piloted with 30 or more analysts in two to three agencies; **P2** five years of budget allocations as open data; an open **BanglaPolicyQA** benchmark; training for 100 officials; a public transparency register. A small, tested reference implementation (research prototype) is released with the Blueprint.

**Safeguards.** Public, officially published data first; no personal data without consent, a DPIA and ethics approval; no sensitive personal data. Advice, not decisions: a person reviews every output and no system decides about individuals. No surveillance, social scoring or profiling of opinions. Risk tiers and lifecycle gates; hosting in Bangladesh; open publication of code and evaluations.

**Decisions requested.**

| No. | Decision | Responsible | Timing |
|----|------------------------------------------------|----------------------|------------|
| D1 | Accept the Blueprint as a technical input to national AI policy drafting | ICT Division | Q4 2026 |
| D2 | Approve a 12-month Phase 1 pilot with a named focal point and a host-institution MoU | ICT Division, host institution | 90 days |
| D3 | Authorise bulk access to already-public document collections; nominate custodians | Custodian agencies | 120 days |
| D4 | Nominate Steering Board members; agree independent open selection of ERRC members | ICT Division, host institution | 90 days |
| D5 | Commission the feasibility and costing study that sets the Phase 2 budget | ICT Division or a partner | Month 6 |
| D6 | Confirm guardrails: no deployment beyond advisory use without a G4 decision; no prohibited uses | Steering Board | At charter |

**Targets for month 12.** Policy search pilot passing its gate with 30 or more active users and Recall@10 of at least 0.85 on the benchmark's private test set; 500 benchmark questions released; five fiscal years of budget allocations published as open data; 100 officials trained; every pilot listed in the transparency register with a model card; a public Year 1 report, including what did not work.

**Key risks and mitigations.** *Data access delays:* start with documents already online. *Wrong answers:* extractive answers with mandatory citations, abstention and human review. *Mission creep:* prohibited uses written into the charter and an independent veto. *Political change:* university host and alignment with the national AI policy process.

**Long-term path (Blueprint Chapter 16).** Pilots and governance proven with public data (2027 to 2028); tools adopted by agencies and aligned with the next national AI policy cycle (2029 to 2030); a national policy-intelligence network on shared standards (2031 to 2035); evidence services embedded in policy cycles by 2041, with charter renewal only if a 2041 review confirms public value. Targets are reviewed in 2027, 2028, 2029, 2030, 2035 and 2041.

**Resources.** About 9 full-time staff in Year 1, rising to 19; two to three servers and one or two inference GPUs. Indicative budget: about Tk 20.4 crore over 36 months (Tk 4.7 crore in Year 1; scenarios Tk 17.9 to 25.3 crore), including escalation and contingency. A planning estimate, not approved funding; confirmed by the month-6 costing study.
