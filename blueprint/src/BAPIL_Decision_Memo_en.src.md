# Decision Memo: A 12-Month Pilot of an AI Policy-Intelligence Capability for Bangladesh

**For the consideration of:** the ICT Division; for information the Planning Commission, Finance Division, Legislative and Parliamentary Affairs Division, BBS and a2i · **From:** Md Naim Hassan Saykat, author of the BAPIL Technical Blueprint · **Date:** {{DATE}}  
**Basis:** BAPIL Technical Blueprint, Version 1.0 ({{DOI_SHORT}}; <https://github.com/md-naim-hassan-saykat/bapil>). This memo is a separate document, not part of the Blueprint.

**Status.** An independent research proposal, not an official document. No authority has decided anything on BAPIL; no body, host or funding exists.

**Purpose and why now.** To propose six decisions that would start, after a preparatory Phase 0, a small, safe and open pilot of an AI tool that helps officials find and cite official evidence in Bangla and English. Drafting of a national AI policy is to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026 [91], after a revised draft of February 2026 [125]; the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 and the National Data Management Act, 2026 now frame public-sector data use [163, 164]; and official documents remain scattered, often as scanned Bangla PDFs.

**Proposal.** A university-anchored lab under an MoU with the ICT Division, with a Steering Board and an independent Ethics and Risk Review Committee (ERRC) whose veto binds the host as far as its statutes and the host agreement allow. Year 1 delivers **P1**, a bilingual search tool that cites official sources or abstains, piloted with 30 or more analysts; **P2**, five years of budget allocations as open data; a 500-question open Bangla benchmark; training for 100 officials; and a public transparency register. *Safeguards:* published data first; no profiling of individuals; other personal data only with consent, a DPIA and ERRC approval; a person reviews every output; hosting in Bangladesh.

**Decisions proposed** (summarised from Blueprint Table 57; bodies and timings are proposals).

| No. | Decision | Proposed responsible body | Proposed timing |
|----|------------------------------------------------|----------------------|------------|
| D1 | Accept the Blueprint as a technical input to national AI policy drafting | ICT Division | Q4 2026 |
| D2 | After Phase 0: approve the 12-month pilot, agree an MoU with the selected host and commit Year 1 funding (about Tk 4.7 crore) | ICT Division and host | End of Phase 0 |
| D3 | Authorise bulk access to already-public document collections; nominate data custodians | LPAD, Finance Division, Planning Commission, BBS, a2i | During Phase 0 |
| D4 | Nominate Steering Board members; select ERRC members by open call through an independent panel | ICT Division and host; independent panel | During Phase 0 |
| D5 | Fund Phase 0 (about Tk 0.44 crore, about six months): open host call, independent legal review, costing study, data-access confirmation; name a focal point | ICT Division or a development partner | Within 90 days |
| D6 | Confirm guardrails: no deployment beyond class A without a G4 decision; no prohibited uses | Steering Board | At charter adoption |

**Sequence and resources.** D1 and D5 come first; D2 only after Phase 0, so Month 1 can be July 2027. Indicative budget: about Tk 20.4 crore over 36 months (Tk 4.7 crore in Year 1; scenarios Tk 17.9 to 25.3 crore; adjusted Tk 22.2 crore), plus Tk 0.44 crore for Phase 0; about 9 full-time staff in Year 1, rising to 19. Planning estimates, not approved funding; the conditional scale-up (Annex W) is not requested.

**Evidence required before D2.** Legal review of the provisions of the 2026 laws in force (U1 to U3); the host’s acceptance of the ERRC veto (U15); costing of omitted items (U16); reuse terms or bulk access (D3).

**Month-12 targets (not results).** P1 passes its gate (30 or more users, Recall@10 at least 0.85, citation precision at least 0.95, usefulness at least 4.0 of 5); 500 benchmark questions; five years of budget data; 100 officials trained.

**Key risks.** Data access (narrower corpus, assisted download); wrong answers (citations, abstention, human review); mission creep (charter prohibitions, independent veto); funding delay (only Phase 0 funded before D2).
